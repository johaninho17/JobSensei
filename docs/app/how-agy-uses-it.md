# How Agy uses your data

Agy gets only what a task needs, not everything you have. Each skill below is a set of instructions that ships with the app.

## Application drafts

**When:** a job reaches the front of the queue.
<!-- feature:!candidates -->
**Agy reads:** the posting, the rows of your work records that match it (usually about twenty), your base resume's layout, your writing voice, and your preferences.
**It writes:** a tailored resume, a cover letter, and a fit rating against the posting's own requirements.
**What helps most:** work records with clear dates and results; a base resume in the shape you want to send.
- Bullets that already fit stay as they are; the rest are reworded from your records, never invented.
- Your current role is written as current; earlier roles and earlier phases as past.
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:candidates -->
**Agy reads:** the posting, the lines from the open candidate's files and your notes that match it, their base resume's layout, and your instructions for Agy (Settings) with the candidate's own (Profile → Settings), which win over yours. Only that candidate's folder; never another candidate's.
**It writes:** a tailored resume, a cover letter only when letters are on and the candidate has enough for one, and a fit rating against the posting's own requirements.
**What helps most:** notes with where and when ("at Northstar Labs since 2022…"); a base resume in the shape you want to send.
- At the current job, three or four bullets are reworded for the posting and the rest kept; earlier jobs stay as written.
- The truth rules sit above your instructions: wording may be stronger, but nothing the files don't state is added.
<!-- /feature -->

## Fit rating

Each requirement from the posting is rated against your records: met, close, learnable, or a gap. Must-haves you lack lower the score but never block you, unless they're legal or location blockers.
<!-- feature:applicationQuestions -->

## Written application questions

**When:** right after an application run starts, for Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever postings.
**Agy reads:** the questions, the posting, that job's evidence, your stories and voice.
**It writes:** short answers in your words; logistics questions are left for you.
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:interviews -->

## Interview prep

**When:** you ask for a round (Prepare … on the job or in the Agy panel).
**Agy reads:** the posting, your evidence (fresh if your records changed since the application), your story bank, interview voice, role memory, and earlier rounds.
**It writes:** a question bank and prep notes. About half the questions are the common ones; the rest are harder ones built from the posting's own work: one specific example under constraints, a depth question on the hardest requirement, a failure scenario, a customer problem you turned into a change. Each experience answer opens with what happened and ends with how it turned out; a story map shows which story answers which kind of question.
**What helps most:** a story bank where each story says what happened, roughly when, and how it ended; recordings of real or practice interviews.
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:jobSearch -->

## Job search

**When:** Run now, or on your schedule (Search settings).
**Agy reads:** your search settings only (titles, places, level, pay floor, words to leave out). Never your resume or records.
**It finds:** companies hiring for your titles; the app then reads their job boards itself. A quick rating of the best matches uses a short summary of your roles and skills.
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:today -->

## Your Today answers

**When:** about a minute after you answer a Today question, and after the morning search.
**Agy reads:** your answers and the list of your work record files.
**It does:** puts each answer into the record it belongs to (your current job, an earlier one, education, or interview stories), keeping your exact limits. Drafts then use it from there.
<!-- /feature -->

## What runs in the background

These run without the Agy panel open, and keep going when you switch pages. The ones that use Agy count against your Agy quota; the rest are the app's own work and cost nothing.

<!-- feature:!candidates -->
| What | When it runs | Uses Agy |
|---|---|---|
| Application drafts | Each job you add, one at a time, when it reaches the front of the queue. "Run automatically" pauses the queue | Yes, in the Agy panel's conversation |
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:candidates -->
| What | When it runs | Uses Agy |
|---|---|---|
| Application drafts | Each job in a candidate's queue, one at a time. When the open candidate's queue is done, the app opens the next candidate with jobs waiting | Yes, in that candidate's own conversation |
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:applicationQuestions -->
| Written application questions | Right after an application run starts, for Greenhouse, Ashby, and Lever postings | Yes, a separate background conversation per job |
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:jobSearch -->
| Job search | Run now, or once a day on your schedule: the first time the computer is on after your set time. Missed while the app was closed, it runs when the app opens or the computer wakes | Yes, for one search of up to five web searches (your schedule can skip Agy on some days); reading companies' job lists doesn't |
| Watched companies | With every search, and alone with Check watched companies | No |
| Light rating | After a search, up to 10 of the best new matches in one call; or Rate with Agy | Yes, one call |
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:today -->
| Filing your Today answers | About a minute after you answer, when the app opens with answers waiting, and after the morning search | Yes, one call for all waiting answers |
| Closed postings | Once a day: when Today opens and after the morning search | No |
| Morning summary | After a scheduled search | No |
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:contentRefresh -->
| Interview content update | Only when you press Update from new recordings | Yes |
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:interviews -->
| Interview prep | Only when you ask for a round | Yes |
<!-- /feature -->

Nothing here sends your files anywhere except to Agy for the task named, and each task gets only what it needs (see the sections above).

## What never uses Agy

<!-- feature:!candidates -->
Reading job boards, checking whether postings closed, quick fit scores, Setup checks, and Learnings are the app's own work, with no AI.
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:candidates -->
Reading postings, suggesting which candidates a posting fits, the Review screen, Mark applied, the Pipeline, and Reports are the app's own work, with no AI.
<!-- /feature -->
