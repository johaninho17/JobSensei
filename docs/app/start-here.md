# Start here

JobSensei tailors applications from your own files: your base resume, your career records, and the job posting. Agy, the AI that writes the drafts, only uses what you give it, so the more real detail it has, the stronger and more accurate the drafts are.

## The minimum

1. **Your name and file prefix** (Setup). They go on every document.
2. **A base resume** as Word (.docx) or Markdown (.md). Every tailored resume keeps its layout, sections, and length. A PDF alone can't be read; keep the Word or Markdown copy beside it.
3. **Agy signed in.** Open the Agy panel once and sign in. If Agy asks you to pick a colour scheme or agree to its terms, switch the panel to **Console** to see and answer it.

## What makes it much better

- **Work records** (Career context → Add files → Work records). One file per job or project with what you did, when, and how it turned out. These are the facts drafts are built from; everything else only adds background.
- **When things happened.** Say which work belongs to your current role and which was earlier (a role change inside the same company counts). Drafts and interview answers then tell your current work as "now" and the rest in order.
- **Other resumes and your LinkedIn export** (Career context → Resumes). They confirm dates and older roles.
- **A writing voice** (Setup). A few lines on how you write; letters and answers then sound like you.
- **Names to keep private** (Setup). Clients and partners are described, never named, in anything you send.
<!-- feature:interviews -->
- **A story bank** (Career context → Story bank). Six to eight real stories, each opening with what happened and ending with how it turned out. Interview prep picks from these.
- **Recordings of interviews and practice interviews** (your `debrief` folder, then Interviews → Your content). They teach the app how you speak, which questions you find hard, and new stories.

## A good routine

1. Add a job (a link or the pasted posting). Agy drafts the resume and a cover letter and rates your fit.
2. Read the drafts on the job page, edit if needed, and export PDFs.
3. Keep the status current (Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Closed) so the app learns what works.
<!-- feature:today -->
4. Check **Today** each morning: new matches, what's due, and questions whose answers make your records stronger.
