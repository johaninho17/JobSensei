# Start here

<!-- feature:!candidates -->
JobSensei tailors applications from your own files: your base resume, your career records, and the job posting. Agy, the AI that writes the drafts, only uses what you give it, so the more real detail it has, the stronger and more accurate the drafts are.

## The minimum

1. **Your name and file prefix** (Setup). They go on every document.
2. **A base resume** as Word (.docx) or Markdown (.md). Every tailored resume keeps its layout, sections, and length. A PDF alone can't be read; keep the Word or Markdown copy beside it.
3. **Agy signed in.** Open the Agy panel once and sign in. If Agy asks you to pick a colour scheme or agree to its terms, switch the panel to **Console** to see and answer it.

## What makes it much better

- **Work records** (Career context → Add files → Work records). One file per job or project with what you did, when, and how it turned out. These are the facts drafts are built from; everything else only adds background.
- **When things happened.** Say which work belongs to your current role and which was earlier (a role change inside the same company counts). Drafts and interview answers then tell your current work as "now" and the rest in order.
- **Other resumes and your LinkedIn export** (Career context → Resumes). They confirm dates and older roles.
- **A writing voice** (Setup). A few lines on how you write; letters and answers then sound like you.
- **Names to keep private** (Setup). Clients and partners are described, never named, in anything you send.

JobSensei Recruiter Edition tailors resumes for your candidates from their own files: their base resume, anything else you have for them, your notes, and the job posting. Agy, the AI that writes the drafts, only uses what you give it for that candidate. Each candidate's files and Agy history are kept apart, so nothing from one candidate reaches another's drafts.

## The minimum

1. **Agy signed in, once.** Open the Agy panel and sign in with your account; every candidate uses it. If Agy asks you to pick a colour scheme or agree to its terms, switch the panel to **Console** to see and answer it.
2. **A candidate with a base resume** (Candidates → Add candidate). The resume as Word (.docx), Markdown (.md), or a PDF with selectable text; every tailored resume keeps its sections and length. After adding a PDF, check Profile → Resume → What JobSensei read.

## What makes it much better

- **Your notes** (Profile → Notes). One fact per line, with where and when: "At Northstar Labs since 2022, owns about 40 mid-market accounts." Drafts can use each line as written.
- **Other files** (Profile → Files and Other resumes): older resumes, a LinkedIn export, work samples. Every checked file counts as evidence for that candidate.
- **The brief** (Profile → Overview → Brief): target roles, places, work setup, pay floor, companies to skip, a weekly goal, and whether they approve each job first. Postings use it to suggest who a job fits.
- **Application facts** (Profile → Overview): email, phone, work authorization, pay expectation, start date. Shown on each job with Copy buttons while you fill the form; never sent to Agy.
- **Your defaults and instructions** (Settings): cover letters on or off, resume summary, PDF size, and instructions for Agy for every candidate. A candidate's own choices and instructions go in Profile → Settings.
- **Cover letters** are off unless you turn them on. A letter is written only when the candidate also has a career story, a writing voice, and two or more files besides the resume.
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:interviews -->
- **A story bank** (Career context → Story bank). Six to eight real stories, each opening with what happened and ending with how it turned out. Interview prep picks from these.
- **Recordings of interviews and practice interviews** (your `debrief` folder, then Interviews → Your content). They teach the app how you speak, which questions you find hard, and new stories.

## A good routine

<!-- feature:!candidates -->
1. Add a job (a link or the pasted posting). Agy drafts the resume and a cover letter and rates your fit.
2. Read the drafts on the job page, edit if needed, and export PDFs.
3. Keep the status current (Applied, Interviewing, Offer, Closed) so the app learns what works.
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:candidates -->
1. Add postings on **Postings** and choose the candidates each one is for, or add a job inside a candidate's **Jobs** to tailor it for them alone.
2. Open the job and **Review** it: the posting's must-haves beside the tailored resume, each changed line with the file it came from. Edit if needed.
3. Apply on the employer's site, then **Mark applied**. The exact PDFs are frozen with the date and where they went, so you can always show what was sent.
4. Keep the status current. **Reports** turns it into a client update, and shows jobs waiting for a candidate's approval.
<!-- /feature -->
<!-- feature:profileQuestions -->
4. When **Profile** shows **Questions for you**, answer them: each answer updates your career records.
