# Stage 02: Hiring Manager

**Round ID:** `02-hiring-manager` · 30–45 min · The functional manager, practice lead, or director.
Card format, framing, gap questions, and limits are owned by the pipeline `SKILL.md`. This file only defines what this round covers.

## What the manager is checking

How the candidate actually operates: ownership, judgment under ambiguity, handling pushback, balancing urgent and long-term work, and working across teams. Managers often ask "give me a real case if you have one, otherwise walk me through your approach." Every card therefore offers a **Proof** (story ID) and **Your approach**.

## Primary question archetypes (8–10)

1. Career walkthrough for a manager: why you chose this kind of work and how you operate.
2. An engagement, project, or customer you owned end to end.
3. A customer or stakeholder pushed back on a constraint, design, or timeline. How did you reach alignment?
4. Being the go-between: translating between customers and engineering (or the equivalent teams in the role's field).
5. Working across teams to get something built or changed for a customer.
6. Balancing support, escalations, and interruptions against planned longer-term work.
7. Ambiguous or shifting requirements.
8. Working within constraints the JD names (data ownership, security or compliance, budget, regulation), at the level the candidate actually has.
9. Something that did not go as planned and what you changed.
10. A JD-based hypothetical framed generically ("a customer needs help using an API they don't understand"), answered with a real case if one exists, otherwise the approach.

About half of these are pressure questions (pipeline `SKILL.md` § Question mix). For a hiring manager the strongest are: a compound example (2 with several stakeholders and a deadline), pain to product (5 as one specific customer problem that became a change), a failure-mode scenario in the posting's domain (8 or 10), a depth probe on the hardest must-have, and what you'd do differently (9).

`questionsToAsk`: 4–5 manager questions (what success looks like in 6 months, how work reaches the team, biggest current challenge, how the team works with product or engineering, next steps).

## `prep.json` sections

1. `beforeYouStart` and `learnBeforeInterview` (pipeline-owned). Each item below is one `sections` entry, its heading as `heading`.
2. `## How I work`: 3–4 points of operating principles drawn from the candidate's real stories, each with a story ID.
3. `## My stories for this round`: the story IDs chosen for this round (none used in Round 01), each with one cue and its framing limit.
4. `## First 30-60-90 days`: realistic, short, learning-first for any gap areas.

## Not in this round

Invented crises or outages, scripted STAR monologues, and lectures about the company's internal systems.
