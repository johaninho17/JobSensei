# Company research (`interviews/company_research.md`)

Written once per job, reused by every round. Short and useful during prep, not an encyclopedia. Every fact line ends with a **specific** source URL (a page, not a homepage) and an access date, or with `(inferred)`. `interview:check` rejects homepage-only sources and "reported" claims without a link.

## Sections, in order

1. `## The role decoded`: what this team does day to day and who it serves, in 3–5 plain bullets drawn from the JD's responsibilities (cite the JD as `original_jd.md`).
2. `## Requirement match`: a table with one row per item in `job.requiredItems`:

   | Requirement | Fit | Your evidence | How to say it |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | <item> | met / learnable / gap | S2 or evidence ID | 3–8 word cue |

   Use the evaluation's must-have status when it exists. Never mark "met" without a real story or evidence ID.
3. `## Words to know`: 5–10 terms from the JD's stack or domain (e.g. a platform's scripting language, a product category, a regulation), each with a one-line plain definition and a public docs link. These are study items, not things the candidate claims to have used.
4. `## Pay`: the posted range from the JD, word for word, or "Not posted".
5. `## What they sell and who buys it`: 2–3 lines.
6. `## Recent news`: up to 3 dated items, each linking the specific article.
7. `## Competitors`: one line.
8. `## Interview process`: what is public for this company and level, with links. Candidate reports are anecdotal: at most three, each linked.
