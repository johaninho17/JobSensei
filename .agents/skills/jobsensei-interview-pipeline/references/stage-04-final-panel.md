# Stage 04: Final Panel

**Round ID:** `04-final-panel` · 30–60 min · Leadership, founders, or a cross-functional panel.
Card format, framing, gap questions, and limits are owned by the pipeline `SKILL.md`. This file only defines what this round covers.

## What the panel is checking

Fit with the company's direction, accountability and learning from mistakes, working across functions, and clear communication of risk and trade-offs. Speak about impact and judgment, not low-level mechanics or resume repetition.

## `question_bank.md` primary archetypes (6–8)

1. Why this company and this direction for your career, longer term.
2. A time you were wrong or changed your mind.
3. Delivering bad news or a risk to a senior stakeholder.
4. Resolving friction between teams with different goals.
5. Short-term customer demands vs. longer-term product or business health.
6. What you would want to learn or build here in your first year.
7. What you expect from your manager and team (culture fit, honestly).

About half are pressure questions (pipeline `SKILL.md` § Question mix): 2 and 3 as one specific case with what changed, 4 as a compound example, plus what you'd do differently.

`## Questions to ask`: 3–4 leadership questions (where the company is heading, the bets behind it, what would make this hire a success, how the team will change).

## `prep.md` sections

1. `## Before you start` and `## Learn before the interview` (pipeline-owned).
2. `## Where I'm heading`: 2–3 points tying the candidate's real direction to the company's public direction.
3. `## My stories for this round`: story IDs not used in Rounds 01–03.

## Not in this round

Round 01 logistics, the elevator pitch again, code-level detail, and corporate clichés.
