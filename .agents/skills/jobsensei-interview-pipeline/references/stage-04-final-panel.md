# Stage 04: Final Panel & Executive Specification

**Stage ID:** `04-final-panel`  
**Stage Name:** Final Panel & Executive Interview  
**Duration & Format:** 30–45 minute executive/leadership interview via Zoom  
**Interviewer Persona:** VP of Engineering, VP of Product/Solutions, Chief Technology Officer, Co-Founder, or Cross-Functional Executive  

---

## 1. Stage Objective & Scope

The Final Panel evaluates high-level alignment, intellectual humility, cultural contribution, and strategic business awareness:
1. Long-term alignment with the company's multi-year mission and market direction.
2. Intellectual humility: taking accountability, receiving critical feedback, and learning from missteps.
3. Cross-functional leadership and scaling philosophy during growth.
4. Executive communication: distilling complex technical realities into clear business trade-offs.

**The Golden Rule of Round 04:** Speak as a strategic peer and future organizational leader. Avoid getting bogged down in low-level code mechanics or repeating standard resume bullet points. Focus on macro business impact, culture add, and strategic trade-offs.

---

## 2. Artifact Layout & Content Archetype

This round produces **The Strategic Vision & Culture Alignment Guide**:
- `interviews/04-final-panel/prep.md`: Executive strategy, company moat analysis, and culture principles.
- `interviews/04-final-panel/question_bank.md`: Bounded strictly to 6–8 strategic questions.
- Company-wide background belongs once at `interviews/company_research.md`. Do not duplicate company research inside this folder.

### `prep.md` Required Sections:
1. **Strategic Positioning & Mission Alignment (45 seconds):** Long-term career vision, industry market trends, and why the candidate is uniquely aligned with this company's competitive trajectory.
2. **Competitive Moat & Market Analysis:** Deep synthesis of the company's market position, customer pain points, and defensibility against competitors (drawn from `company_research.md`).
3. **Intellectual Humility & Operating Principles:** How the candidate operates under uncertainty, learns from mistakes, and maintains cross-functional empathy.
4. **Executive Reverse Questions:** 4–6 high-level questions on the company's 2-to-3-year roadmap, competitive moat evolution, and strategic bets.

---

## 3. `question_bank.md` Structure & Format Standard

The question bank must contain **strictly 6–8 primary questions**:
1. Strategic mission alignment and why this company is positioned to win long-term.
2. An experience where you were wrong or had to fundamentally revise a strongly held conviction (intellectual humility).
3. Maintaining high execution standards and cross-functional empathy during rapid growth.
4. Communicating high-stakes risk or bad news to executive stakeholders clearly and proactively.
5. Cross-functional alignment: resolving friction when engineering, product, and sales incentives clash.
6. Balancing short-term tactical client demands against long-term architectural and business defensibility.
7. Executive Reverse Questions (multi-year vision, strategic bets, market positioning).

### Primary Question Card Format:
```markdown
### Question <N>: <Strategic / Culture Prompt>?

* **Core Takeaway:**
  - One concise sentence summarizing the strategic philosophy or leadership principle.
* **Interviewer Intent:**
  - What the executive is testing (e.g., intellectual humility, strategic foresight, organizational empathy).
* **Strategic Mental Model:**
  - Macro business context and alignment principle.
  - Accountability / decision criteria.
* **Truthful Career Anchor:**
  - Ground in candidate's real verified experience without embellishment.
* **Glanceable Talking Cues:**
  - Punchy 1-line talking trigger 1.
  - Punchy 1-line talking trigger 2.
  - Punchy 1-line talking trigger 3.
* **Truth Boundary & Cautions:**
  - Specific boundary on executive positioning.
```

---

## 4. Strict Prohibitions
- **NO Low-Level Mechanical Trivia:** Never spend executive time discussing syntax, specific compiler flags, or trivial tooling details.
- **NO Basic Recruiter Pitches:** Do not repeat the Round 01 logistics or elevator pitch.
- **NO Corporate Jargon / Slop:** Avoid clichés like "synergy", "paradigm shift", or "rockstar"; speak with grounded, authentic clarity.
