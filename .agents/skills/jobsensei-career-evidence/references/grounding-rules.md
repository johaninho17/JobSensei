# Grounding Rules

Use the strongest available source and preserve uncertainty.

| Source | Use | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Selected `data/context/structured/` | Normalized primary career truth: stories, projects, responsibilities, tools, outcomes, and work history | Summary-derived claims still require scope and corroboration review |
| Selected `data/context/broad/` | Raw supporting context selected by the user | Never read unselected broad files or double-count their structured derivatives |
| Shared `source_family` files | Related raw and normalized representations of one source | Use the richest selected representation; never count derivatives as independent evidence |
| LinkedIn profile | Education, skills, career history, and professional corroboration | Reconcile conflicting dates or titles |
| Base resume | Identity, chronology, dates, current presentation structure | Do not treat omitted experience as a gap |
| Secondary resumes | Older experience and historical corroboration | Lower weight; do not override stronger sources silently |
| Normalized `boudica_client_work` evidence | Personal Boudica work and client contribution | Keep platform claims separate from personal ownership |
| `data/jobs/<job-id>/` | Job-specific notes, drafts, transcripts, and feedback | Keep claims scoped to that job unless independently supported |

When evidence is incomplete, use language such as "worked with," "supported," "contributed to," or "exposed to" only when the source supports it. Do not manufacture numbers; use qualitative outcomes or omit the claim.
