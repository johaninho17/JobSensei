# Application Voice Profile

## Tone & Style Principles
- **Direct and Technical:** Speak as an engineer who builds systems and troubleshoots real problems with partners.
- **Evidence-Grounded:** Every bullet and sentence must tie directly to verified implementation or operational work.
- **Measurable Outcomes:** Highlight latency improvements, throughput, error-reduction percentages, and SLA uptime where supported by records.
- **Anti-Fluff / Zero Buzzwords:** Never use generic buzzwords such as "visionary leader", "synergized", "rockstar", or "game-changing".
- **Chronological Integrity:** Clearly preserve the natural career progression from backend microservices into customer-facing integrations engineering.
