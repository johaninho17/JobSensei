# Candidate Claim Boundaries & Denylist

## Strict Non-Negotiable Boundaries
- **No Hallucinated Credentials:** Never claim banking, FinTech regulatory licenses (e.g. Series 7, Series 63, FINRA), or certified compliance accreditations (SOC2 auditor, PCI-DSS QSA).
- **No Exaggerated Tech Stacks:** Do not claim deep production development in C++, Rust, or Solidity since candidate experience is grounded in TypeScript, Node.js, Python, and SQL.
- **No Fictional Team Leadership:** Do not describe collaborative partner troubleshooting as "managing" or "directing" engineering departments.
- **No Unbacked Metrics:** Only cite latency reductions, transaction volumes, and SLA metrics explicitly documented in structured evidence.
