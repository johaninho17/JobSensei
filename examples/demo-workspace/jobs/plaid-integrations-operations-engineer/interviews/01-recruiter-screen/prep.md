# Recruiter Screen Preparation — Plaid IOE

## Candidate Introduction Pitch (2-Minute Narrative)
"I’m a lead integration engineer with over eight years of experience specializing in high-throughput API architecture, webhook reliability, and partner-facing operational delivery.

For the past three and a half years, I’ve served as Lead Solutions & Integrations Engineer at Meridian Cloud Systems. I architect and maintain the adapter layer connecting our platform to 25+ external partner REST APIs, processing over 45M monthly transactions at 99.99% uptime. A significant part of my day involves solving complex distributed connectivity issues: diagnosing schema drift, designing Redis-backed webhook retry pipelines with full jitter, and leading technical triage with partner engineering teams when syncs fail.

Prior to Meridian, I spent three years at Kinetics Data Platform building streaming ingestion microservices and tuning PostgreSQL pipelines to cut query latency by 45%, following foundational REST development at Cascade Software Labs after graduating from UC Berkeley with my degree in EECS.

What excites me about Plaid’s Integrations Operations Engineering team is the sheer scale and criticality of the mission. When an integration has a defect at Plaid, it affects thousands of downstream applications and millions of end-users connecting their financial accounts. I want to bring my eight years of integration debugging and operational resilience to keeping Plaid's financial network rock-solid."

## Key Themes to Reinforce
1. **Seniority & Calm Under Pressure:** 8+ years debugging production outages across external enterprise boundaries.
2. **Proactive Reliability:** Designing synthetic probes and automatic retry engines so problems are caught before customers notice.
3. **Cross-Organizational Diplomacy:** Providing reproducible cURL scripts, exact payload diffs, and structured logs to partner engineers.
