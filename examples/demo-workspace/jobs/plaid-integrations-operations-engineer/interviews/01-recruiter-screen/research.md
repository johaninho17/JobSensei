# Plaid Company & Team Research

## Company Overview
- **Founded:** 2013 by Zach Perret and William Hockey; Headquartered in San Francisco, CA.
- **Mission:** Democratize financial services by building the data and payment rails that thousands of fintech apps and banks rely on.
- **Core Network:** Connects over 12,000 financial institutions (Chase, Wells Fargo, community banks, credit unions) with tens of thousands of digital apps (Venmo, SoFi, Robinhood, Betterment, Chime).

## Core Product Architecture
- **Plaid Link:** The drop-in client-side SDK that guides end-users through selecting their bank and credential authentication.
- **Plaid Auth & Balance:** Instant account number verification and real-time balance checks to prevent ACH overdrafts.
- **Plaid Transactions:** Clean, categorized transaction data history fetched via bank core feeds or secure API connections.
- **Integrations Operations Engineering (IOE):** The frontline engineering organization responsible for network connectivity uptime, bank partner API telemetry, integration defect remediation, and rapid incident response when a bank changes its authentication or payload schema.

## Key Discussion Points for Candidate
- **Business Impact Focus:** Emphasize that resolving an integration bug isn't just closing a ticket—it directly unblocks thousands of daily end-users attempting to transfer funds or connect their accounts.
- **Partner Communication:** Highlight experience talking to external developer teams with empathy, clear reproducibility steps, and structured logging.
